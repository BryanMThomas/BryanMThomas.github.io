Wags privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Wags Personal Assistant Google integration operated by Bryan Thomas for his own use.

Data accessed and purpose

When authorized, Wags accesses Google Calendar metadata and events, including dates, descriptions, locations, and attendee information, to summarize schedules and help manage calendar blocks. If Contacts access is granted, it reads saved contact details, including names, birthdays, and contact information, to support relationship reminders. It requests access through Google’s consent screen.

Processing and sharing

Wags runs on a privately managed computer. Relevant account information may be sent to configured cloud AI providers to produce Bryan’s requested summaries and assistance. Authorized outputs may also be delivered to Bryan through his configured communication tools. These providers process that information under their applicable service terms and privacy policies. Information is not sold or used for advertising. Google data is not used to train a generalized AI model. Human access is limited to Bryan and people he explicitly authorizes for support, except where legally required.

Wags’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the private Wags computer with restricted file permissions. Selected information may appear in local assistant notes, logs, backups, or authorized message history. It is retained while needed for Bryan’s assistant workflows or troubleshooting and can be removed by Bryan. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically erase previously stored notes, messages, logs, backups, or local credential files.

Access controls and deletion

Bryan can review and revoke the integration at Google Account connections. To remove retained integration data, he can delete the relevant Wags credential files, notes, logs, backups, and delivered messages, or request help at the contact below. Existing calendar events remain subject to Google Calendar’s own controls.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: wags@codeandcapital.co. This policy will be updated if the integration’s data practices change.

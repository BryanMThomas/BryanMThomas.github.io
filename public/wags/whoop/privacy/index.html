Wags WHOOP privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Wags WHOOP is a private integration operated by Bryan Thomas for his personal assistant. It is not offered to the public.

Health data accessed

With Bryan’s authorization, Wags reads WHOOP recovery scores, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, daily cycles and strain, sleep, workouts, basic profile information, and body measurements. It uses these data for personal health summaries, activity planning, and reminders. It does not diagnose conditions or replace medical care.

Processing and sharing

Credentials and monitoring run on the privately managed Wags computer. Relevant health information may be processed by configured cloud AI providers and delivered to Bryan through his configured communication tools for authorized assistant tasks. Provider processing is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. Data is not sold or used for advertising. The integration is configured to read WHOOP data, not change it.

Storage and access controls

OAuth credentials are stored privately on Wags with restricted file permissions. Health summaries may remain in local notes, logs, backups, or authorized message history while needed for Bryan’s workflows. Access is limited to Bryan and support he authorizes, except where legally required.

Revocation and deletion

Bryan can disconnect the integration through his WHOOP connected-app settings. Revocation stops future authorized WHOOP access but does not automatically delete retained summaries, credentials, notes, logs, backups, or delivered messages. Bryan can delete those separately or request assistance at wags@codeandcapital.co.

Wags Personal Assistant

Wags is Bryan Thomas’s private personal assistant, running on a privately managed computer using Hermes Agent. It helps Bryan organize his schedule and personal responsibilities. Wags is not a public service and does not offer account registration.

Google account access

With Bryan’s consent, Wags uses Google Calendar to read calendars and events available to his account and help manage calendar blocks. The current Calendar permission permits broader calendar management; Wags’s operating rules limit its actions. Google Contacts read access, when authorized, helps identify birthdays and personal check-ins.

Connecting Google does not authorize Wags to send email, trade, make payments, or contact other people. Google permissions are granted separately from Wags’s operating rules.

Read the privacy policy for information about processing, storage, and revocation.
